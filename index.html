How mastery is assessed

Complete a learning unit only after you can answer its questions, attempt its exercise, and explain at least one limitation. Completion is self-reported, not automatic grading.

  • Each chapter: approximately 14 hours across two weeks.
  • Typical allocation: 4h concepts, 2h demonstrations, 5h implementation, 2h evaluation, 1h review.
  • Projects are included in the 168-hour plan—not extra homework.
  • Capstone: guided integration. Final project: independent problem-solving.
  • Final rubric: framing 15%, implementation 20%, evaluation 25%, reliability/security 20%, user experience 10%, documentation 10%.
  • The schedule is flexible. This is a degree-inspired foundation, not a replacement for a full university degree.

After the foundation

Optional specializations—not included in the 168-hour core: computer vision, multimodal systems, speech, recommendations, reinforcement learning, production fine-tuning and LoRA, quantization, distributed training, advanced MLOps, research-paper reproduction, and advanced AI security.
